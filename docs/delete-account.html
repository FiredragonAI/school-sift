学校通 · SchoolSift 删除账号

只有开启过「家庭同步」的人才有账号。不开家庭同步时,学校通没有账号,数据只在你的设备上。

不用安装应用也能删除

  1. 用浏览器打开网页版 firedragonai.github.io/school-sift(手机、电脑都可以)
  2. 进入「更多 → 家庭同步」,用你的邮箱和密码登录
  3. 拉到页面最下面,点「删除账号」,再输入一次密码确认

在 Android 应用里也是同样的位置:「更多 → 家庭同步 → 删除账号」。忘记密码时,在登录页点「忘记密码」重设后再删除。

会删除什么

  • 登录账号(邮箱和密码凭证):立即永久删除。
  • 家庭的云端数据(孩子资料、通知与照片、日程、待办、成绩、学习记录、英文回信记录):如果你是家庭里唯一的成员,立即一起删除;如果家庭里还有其他成员,这些数据留给他们继续使用,只把你从家庭里移出。
  • 我们不另外保留这些数据的副本。
  • 设备上的数据不受影响:可以在应用里「更多 → 清空全部数据」删除,或直接卸载应用 / 清除浏览器网站数据。

遇到问题

如果无法登录又需要删除账号,请在 github.com/FiredragonAI/school-sift/issues 提交请求(不要在公开页面里写密码)。

更多信息见隐私政策。

Delete your SchoolSift account (English)

You only have an account if you turned on Family sync. Without it, SchoolSift has no account and your data stays on your device.

Without installing the app: open the web version at firedragonai.github.io/school-sift, go to More → Family sync, sign in with your email and password, scroll to the bottom, tap “Delete account” and confirm with your password. The Android app has the same option in the same place. Forgot your password? Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen first.

What is deleted: your sign-in account is deleted immediately and permanently. If you are the only member of your family, the family’s cloud data (children, notices and photos, events, to-dos, grades, study logs, reply history) is deleted at the same time; if other members remain, that data stays with them and you are removed from the family. We keep no separate copy. Data on your device is not affected — use More → Clear all data, uninstall the app, or clear the site’s data in your browser.

If you cannot sign in and need your account deleted, open a request at github.com/FiredragonAI/school-sift/issues (never post your password). See the privacy policy.
